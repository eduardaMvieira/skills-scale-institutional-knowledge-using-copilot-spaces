# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

These documents describe OctoAcme's cross-functional approach to delivering product features, services, and integrations. They provide a shared guide to the project lifecycle, team responsibilities, communication, and quality practices.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme moves work through initiation, planning, iterative execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines outcomes and success measures, and records a go/no-go decision. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, risks, responsibilities, and milestones.

During execution, teams use a project board to track work, deliver in small increments, review pull requests, test changes, demonstrate progress, and monitor delivery metrics. Roles and responsibilities are shared across the Project Manager (who coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications), Product Manager/Product Lead (who defines outcomes and prioritizes work), developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders. Regular team syncs, demos, stakeholder updates, a shared status source, and clear escalation paths support coordination throughout the lifecycle.

Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans. Deployment is followed by verification and stakeholder communication. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team holds a retrospective, records lessons, and tracks a small set of improvement actions with owners and success criteria.
